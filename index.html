For amusement only: Doggie Quarters can't be cashed out and have no value outside the game. The DQ store is a demo and charges nothing. Five reels, ten lines. One coin per line; each coin bet lights one more line (middle, top, bottom, the two V's, then the rest). Line wins pay left to right from the first reel, and all line wins are added together. Three or more DIRT MOUNDS anywhere on screen start the bone dig; three or more mystery DOG HOUSES (the purple ones with a ? on the door) anywhere start the dog house pick bonus; three or more MAILBOXES anywhere start the mailman chase. Every bonus pays a multiple of your total bet. Two bonuses on one spin play one after the other, and the second pays double. DOG MOVES: now and then a dog joins a spin. Koda can dig up the third bonus coin when two are showing; Duke doubles or triples a win; Peanut stamps 2–3 wild paws on the middle reels (a paw stands in for any line symbol, never a bonus coin); Rex shoves a reel one stop to make a win; Biscuit polishes every bone on screen to the best one showing. Each move is drawn with the spin by the same fair random draw. The figures above leave out the dog moves and the golden collar, which only add to them. AUTO spins a run by itself (hold SPIN or tap AUTO) and stops for any bonus or big win; TURBO makes spins quicker. Play money: Doggie Quarters (DQ) are the game's own currency and have no cash value. Demo game for entertainment only — no real money is wagered or paid.
